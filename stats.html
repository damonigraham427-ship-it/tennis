Tennis — Physical Stats of the Environment

Source: ITF Rules of Tennis. SI first, imperial alongside. Lines marked COMPUTED or DERIVED are calculated from the official figures, not official stats.

1. The Ball

Diameter, Type 1 / Type 2 / High Altitude: 6.54–6.86 cm (2.57–2.70 in) — Appendix I

Diameter, Type 3 (slow): 7.00–7.30 cm (2.76–2.87 in) — Appendix I

Weight (mass), all types: 56.0–59.4 g (1.975–2.095 oz) — Appendix I

Rebound, Type 2 (medium, the standard ball): 135–147 cm (53–58 in) when dropped 254 cm (100 in) onto a concrete base — Appendix I

Rebound, Type 1 (fast): 138–151 cm (54–59 in), same drop test — Appendix I

Rebound, Type 3 (slow): 135–147 cm (53–58 in), same drop test — Appendix I

Rebound, High Altitude type: 122–135 cm (48–53 in), same drop test — Appendix I

Forward deformation, all types: 0.56–0.74 cm (0.220–0.291 in) under an 8.165 kg (18 lb) load — Appendix I

Return deformation, Type 2 / Type 3 / High Altitude: 0.80–1.08 cm (0.315–0.425 in) under the same load — Appendix I

Return deformation, Type 1: 0.74–1.08 cm (0.291–0.425 in) — Appendix I

Durability limits (max change after the ITF durability test): mass 0.4 g (0.014 oz); rebound 4.0 cm (1.6 in); forward deformation 0.08 cm (0.031 in); return deformation 0.10 cm (0.039 in) — Appendix I

Outer surface: uniform, consisting of a fabric cover; any seams must be stitchless — Appendix I / Rule 3

Colour: white or yellow only — Appendix I / Rule 3

Construction: a pressurised type has internal pressure greater than the external pressure; pressureless types also exist under the same size/mass envelope — Appendix I

Average density: ≈ 0.37 g/cm³ (≈ 366 kg/m³; ≈ 22.9 lb/ft³), from the midpoint mass (57.7 g) and midpoint diameter (6.70 cm, volume ≈ 157.5 cm³); full range across the official tolerances ≈ 0.33–0.41 g/cm³ — COMPUTED

2. The Court

Length, singles and doubles: 23.77 m (78 ft), baseline to baseline — Rule 1

Width, singles: 8.23 m (27 ft) — Rule 1

Width, doubles: 10.97 m (36 ft) — Rule 1

Doubles alley (extra width each side): 1.37 m (4 ft 6 in) — Rule 1 / Appendix X — DERIVED

Net to baseline (half-court length): 11.89 m (39 ft) — Appendix X

Service line distance from net: 6.40 m (21 ft) on each side, drawn between the singles sidelines, parallel to the net — Rule 1

Service line to baseline: 5.49 m (18 ft) — DERIVED

Service box size (each of the four): 6.40 m deep × 4.115 m wide (21 ft × 13 ft 6 in); the centre service line splits the area between the service lines into two equal boxes — Rule 1 / Appendix X

Centre mark: 10 cm (4 in) long, drawn inside the court from each baseline, in line with the centre service line — Rule 1

Line widths — centre service line and centre mark: 5 cm (2 in) wide — Rule 1

Line widths — all other lines: 2.5–5 cm (1–2 in) wide, except baselines, which may be up to 10 cm (4 in) wide — Rule 1

Measurement convention: all court measurements are made to the outside of the lines; all lines the same colour, clearly contrasting with the surface — Rule 1

Playing area inside the lines: singles 2,106 sq ft (195.7 m²); doubles 2,808 sq ft (260.9 m²) — COMPUTED

Recommended run-back behind each baseline, international competition: 6.40 m (21 ft) minimum — Appendix X (recommendation, not a playing rule)

Recommended side space outside each sideline, international competition: 3.66 m (12 ft) minimum — Appendix X

Recommended total court enclosure, international competition: 36.6 × 18.3 m (120 × 60 ft) — Appendix X

Recommended run-back / side space, recreational and club play: 5.48 m (18 ft) behind baselines; 3.05 m (10 ft) at sides; total 34.7 × 17.1 m (114 × 56 ft) — Appendix X

Surface types (physical facts only): the ITF classifies court surfaces and rates their pace; the main physical families are hard (acrylic over asphalt/concrete), clay (crushed stone/brick), and grass (natural turf), plus artificial grass, artificial clay, carpet and others — ITF court surface classification / Court Pace Rating programme

3. The Net and Posts

Net height at centre: 0.914 m (3 ft) — Rule 1

Net height at posts: 1.07 m (3 ft 6 in) — Rule 1

Centre strap: holds the net down taut at the centre; not more than 5 cm (2 in) wide; completely white — Rule 1

Net band (covering the cord and top of the net): 5–6.35 cm (2–2.5 in) deep on each side; completely white — Rule 1

Net cord / metal cable: maximum diameter 0.8 cm (1/3 in) — Rule 1

Net mesh: small enough that a ball cannot pass through; net fully extended to fill the space between the posts — Rule 1

Doubles net posts: centres 0.914 m (3 ft) outside the doubles court on each side — Rule 1

Singles net posts (if a singles net is used): centres 0.914 m (3 ft) outside the singles court on each side — Rule 1

Singles sticks (used to hold a doubles net up for singles): centres 0.914 m (3 ft) outside the singles court on each side; support the net at 1.07 m (3 ft 6 in); not more than 7.5 cm (3 in) square or in diameter — Rule 1

Net posts size limit: not more than 15 cm (6 in) square or 15 cm (6 in) in diameter — Rule 1

Post tops: net posts and singles sticks not more than 2.5 cm (1 in) above the top of the net cord — Rule 1

4. The Racket

Maximum overall length (frame including handle): 73.7 cm (29.0 in) — Appendix II

Maximum overall width: 31.7 cm (12.5 in) — Appendix II

Hitting surface, maximum length: 39.4 cm (15.5 in), measured parallel to the longitudinal axis of the handle — Appendix II

Hitting surface, maximum width: 29.2 cm (11.5 in), measured perpendicular to the longitudinal axis of the handle — Appendix II

Hitting surface form: flat, a pattern of crossed strings alternately interlaced or bonded where they cross; pattern generally uniform and not less dense in the centre than in any other area; playing characteristics identical on both faces — Appendix II

Not listed — no official figure

Ball internal pressure: the ITF Rules (Appendix I) publish no numeric internal-pressure value for pressurised balls, so no pressure figure is listed.

Ball core material: Appendix I specifies the fabric cover, stitchless seams and colour, but does not specify the core material, so it is not presented as an official stat.
